This isn't one straight ladder — it's a main path plus a side offer that runs everywhere alongside it:

**Path A — Premium 1:1 (the core business):**
Free content → free lead magnet → **The Shadow Initiation ($497)** → **The Shadow Immersion ($797)**

It's promoted continuously and everywhere (email automation, website, social media) as its own low-cost entry, independent of the premium path.
