# The Mystic Soul Vine Academy — Content Funnel Plan
### Turning existing viewers into email subscribers via the two free lead magnets

Purpose: you already have viewers on YouTube and social — this isn't about getting more of them, it's about making sure the ones already there actually land on The Inner Alchemy checklist or The Soul Gift Clarity Workbook instead of just watching and scrolling on.

## The One Decision Rule: Which Magnet, When

Promoting both magnets identically, every time, trains people to tune the ask out. Instead, match the magnet to the content:

| If the video/post is about... | Promote... |
|---|---|
| Signs, synchronicities, repeating numbers, patterns showing up in life, shadow work | **The Inner Alchemy checklist** |
| Feeling scattered, multi-passionate identity, "which gift do I build around," direction/purpose | **The Soul Gift Clarity Workbook** |
| Anything that touches both, or you're not sure | Default to **The Inner Alchemy checklist** — it's the simpler, single-concept ask and an easier yes for someone who doesn't know you well yet. |

## Where the CTA Needs to Live (platform by platform)

- **YouTube video description** — already done (both magnets listed in `mystic-soul-vine-brand.md`).
- **YouTube end screen** (last 5-20 seconds of the video) — add a card/end screen pointing to whichever magnet matches that video's topic. This is the single highest-leverage thing you're likely not doing yet — most viewers never scroll down to the description.
- **Pinned comment** on every video — template below.
- **YouTube Community tab** — a weekly reminder post, separate from the video itself.
- **Instagram bio link** — needs to be a single landing page (Linktree, Stan Store, or similar) listing both magnets, the Facebook community, Cosmic Shadow, and the application link — Instagram only allows one bio link, so it has to do all the work at once. If you don't have one of these set up yet, that's worth doing before anything else in this plan.
- **Instagram/Facebook posts** — repurpose each Wednesday video into a caption/carousel that ends in the matching magnet, not a generic "check out my new video."
- **Facebook group** — pinned welcome post with both magnet links, plus an occasional reflection-question post.

## Verbal CTA Script (say near the end of each video, before your sign-off)

**When the video matches The Inner Alchemy checklist:**
> "If what I just shared feels familiar — if you've had a sign like this and brushed past it — I made something for exactly this. It's called The Inner Alchemy checklist, it's free, and the link is in the description and pinned comment below. Go grab it before you do anything else today."

**When the video matches The Soul Gift Clarity Workbook:**
> "If you've ever felt like you're carrying five gifts and no idea which one to build your life around — that's exactly what the Soul Gift Clarity Workbook is for. It's free, it's linked below, and it's the first real step toward stopping the scattering."

Keep the verbal CTA to one, specific, and placed in the same spot in every video (right before your closing) — viewers learn where to expect it, which makes them more likely to act on it instead of being caught off guard.

## Weekly Rhythm (built around your existing Wednesday video)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Wednesday | Publish the video. Add matching end screen + pinned comment (above). Post to YouTube Community tab announcing it. |
| Thursday | Post an Instagram/Facebook caption or carousel repurposing the video's core idea, ending in the matching magnet link. |
| Saturday | Instagram Story reminder — one slide, one line ("Still haven't grabbed this? 👇"), sticker link to the matching magnet. |
| Sunday | Facebook group post — a reflection question related to the week's topic (not a hard sell), with the magnet link mentioned naturally in the comments if people ask "where do I learn more about this." |

(No hard CTA in the post itself — the free community should feel like a place to be witnessed, not another sales channel. If someone asks where to learn more in the comments, that's when you point them to the matching magnet.)
