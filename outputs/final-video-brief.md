## Sharpened Audience (was: 3, generic category)

Not "coaches and consultants." The specific viewer: **a solo expert who already has an offer or a service, has tried at least three AI tools in the last six months, and has nothing to show for it except a longer app list.** They don't need to be convinced AI matters — they need to stop feeling behind and get one working system instead of another tab.

## Sharpened Urgency (was: 3, understated)

The pain isn't "AI is confusing." It's this: **every week spent evaluating tools is a week a competitor spends shipping.** The cost isn't confusion — it's the compounding gap between "has a system" and "has a tool graveyard." This video's job is to close that gap in 14 minutes on screen, visibly.

## Sharpened Lead Magnet (was: 3, "a template" undersells it)

**Reframe:** not "download my template" — **"clone my agent team."** The download isn't a PDF or a checklist; it's the literal working files that just ran on screen. The pull isn't information, it's possession of the exact mechanism the viewer watched produce a real result. Naming it "The Revenue Agent Stack" (not "the template") ties it directly to the mechanism established mid-video, so the ask at the end feels like the obvious next click, not a pivot into a pitch.

## Sharpened Sales Path (was: 3, generic ladder)

Old: free video → lead magnet → workshop → premium service (says nothing about *why* each step happens).

New: **Watch it work → run it yourself → get stuck customizing it for your specific offer → pay to skip that stuck point.**
That's the real mechanism of the sales path — the free download proves the system works in general; the Workshop exists specifically because *customizing the stack to one person's specific business* is the part people can't do alone. The premium build exists for people who'd rather hand that customization off entirely. Every paid step is sold as solving a specific, named friction point in using the free thing, not as a generic "next tier."
